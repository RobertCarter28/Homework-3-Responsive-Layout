THE BASICS

What Is NIL?

NIL stands for Name, Image, and Likeness. It gives college athletes opportunities to earn money by using their personal brand. Athletes can receive compensation through sponsorships, advertisements, endorsements, social media, and other business opportunities.

THE POSITIVES

Benefits of NIL

01

Financial Opportunities

Athletes can earn money while attending college and participating in sports.

02

Personal Branding

Athletes can build their reputation and connect with businesses and fans.

03

Business Experience

NIL helps athletes learn about marketing, sponsorships, and managing money.

THE DIFFICULTIES

Challenges of NIL

01

Unequal Opportunities

Some athletes receive more sponsorship opportunities than others.

02

Pressure on Athletes

Athletes must balance school, sports, and business responsibilities.

03

Changing College Sports

NIL continues to change recruiting, competition, and college athletics.

LOOKING AHEAD

The Future of NIL

NIL will continue to play an important role in college athletics. As rules and opportunities develop, athletes will have new ways to earn money, grow their brands, and prepare for careers beyond sports.

NIL creates opportunities, but it also brings challenges that colleges and athletes must continue to address.
